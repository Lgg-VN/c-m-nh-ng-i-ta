The 5-Step Intimate Experience

More Than a Cooking Class.
A Day in a Saigon Home.

From the historic produce aisles of Chợ Tân Định to our family kitchen in District 1, experience the true warmth of southern Vietnamese home life.

1 Step One • ~60 Minutes

Tân Định Wet Market Discovery

We meet at Chợ Tân Định, one of District 1's most iconic markets founded in the 1920s. We walk past aromatic herb vendors, tropical vegetables, and fresh catches from the Mekong Delta.

  • Touch & smell herbs: culantro, thai basil, perilla leaves
  • Learn respectful market customs with multi-generational vendors
  • Taste warm sticky rice treats or soy curd along the aisle
Chợ Tân Định, District 1
Our District 1 Home Kitchen
2 Step Two • ~90 Minutes

Hands-on Home Cooking

Take a short stroll to our alley townhouse in District 1. Tie on an apron, drink calamansi cooler, and prepare 4 signature home dishes side-by-side with our family.

  • Master claypot caramelization (Kho Tộ) with cane sugar
  • Balance sweet tamarind broth and crispy wok-tossed greens
  • Hand-pound shallots and peppercorns in traditional stone mortars
3 Step Three • ~75 Minutes

Dine, Connect & "Mời Cơm"

Gather around the iconic family round tray (Mâm Cơm). Experience the heartwarming custom of Mời Cơm—inviting elders and friends to start the meal.

  • Learn communal dining etiquette and chopstick courtesy
  • Heartfelt conversations about Saigon history, family values, and daily customs
  • Generous refills of jasmine rice cooked straight in earthenware pots
The Sacred Family "Mâm Cơm"
4 Step Four • ~45 Minutes

Tea, Seasonal Treats & Daily Customs

Relax after the feast with a warm pot of lotus green tea, tropical fruits (pomelo, dragonfruit, rambutan), and handmade mung bean cakes while chatting like long-time friends.

"The table cleanses, but the tea deepens the friendship. Ask us anything about life in Vietnam."

We strictly use triple-filtered RO water for cooking, stocks, and tea. Individual clay pots are prepared separately for gluten-free, peanut-free, or strict vegan guests without cross-contamination.

Our Saigon Family

Why We Opened Our
Family Kitchen to the World

We are Cô Hương and nephew Minh. Our family has lived in this quiet alley in District 1 for decades. Growing up, our dining table was always filled with laughter, clinking porcelain bowls, and the smell of braised fish caramelized over heat.

International travelers often experience Saigon through restaurant chains. We founded Cơm Nhà Người Ta so visitors could experience what a real Vietnamese domestic meal feels like: eating together, learning about family life, and feeling at home.

Practical Information & FAQs

Everything you need to know before coming to our family table in District 1.

We meet at the main entrance gate of Chợ Tân Định (Hai Bà Trưng Street, Tân Định Ward, District 1). It is easily reachable within 5–10 minutes via Grab car or taxi from Ben Thanh Market or Opera House. We provide live GPS links upon booking.
Dress casually for tropical weather! Flat walking shoes or sneakers with grip are recommended as market walkways can be wet from ice and fresh produce. Clean indoor slippers are provided when entering our home.
We uphold strict hygiene standards because our family eats the exact same meal with you. We use pure RO filtered drinking water for cooking, stocks, and tea. Herbs undergo careful multi-stage clean water rinses.
100% refund for cancellations made up to 24 hours before your session. Tân Định market and our home are fully covered and air-conditioned, so rainy Saigon days run comfortably.
