The 5-Step Intimate Experience

More Than a Cooking Class.
A Day in a Saigon Home.

From the bustling narrow aisles of the morning wet market to our peaceful family courtyard, we take you by the hand through the authentic rhythm of southern Vietnamese domestic life.

1 Step One • ~60 Minutes

Local Wet Market Discovery

We meet at sunrise or afternoon glow at Chợ Vườn Chuối, a traditional neighborhood wet market untouched by mass tourism. Walk through aisles of vibrant herbs like *kinh giới*, *tía tô*, and fresh morning catch.

  • Learn to select sweet shallots, garlic, and ripe lemongrass
  • Understand market bargaining customs with respected local vendors
  • Taste fresh warm soy pudding or coconut wafers along the way
Chợ Vườn Chuối, District 3
Our Home Kitchen Hearth
2 Step Two • ~90 Minutes

Hands-on Home Cooking

Stroll together down the quiet alley to our family townhouse. Put on your apron, pour a refreshing glass of calamansi cooler, and prepare 4 traditional dishes side-by-side with our family cooks.

  • Master the art of claypot caramelization (*Kho Tộ*)
  • Balance the holy quintet of flavors: sweet, salty, sour, bitter, umami
  • Hand-pound herbs in stone mortars with family guidance
3 Step Three • ~75 Minutes

Dine, Connect & "Mời Cơm"

Gather around the iconic round family tray (*Mâm Cơm*). In Vietnam, eating is never solitary; it is an act of union. Experience the heartwarming custom of *Mời Cơm*—inviting elders and friends to start the meal.

  • Learn communal dining etiquette and chopstick courtesy
  • Heartfelt conversations about Saigon history, family values, and everyday life
  • Generous refills of piping hot jasmine rice straight from the pot
The Sacred "Mâm Cơm"
4 Step Four • ~45 Minutes

Tea, Seasonal Treats & Daily Customs

After the feast, we move to the living room or open balcony. Savor artisanal Vietnamese green tea scented with lotus petals, peeled seasonal fruits (sweet rambutan, pomelo, dragonfruit), and traditional mung bean cakes.

"The table cleanses, but the tea deepens the friendship. Ask us anything about life in Vietnam."

The Anatomy of a Home Meal

The Balance of "Mâm Cơm"

Unlike Western multi-course dining, a Vietnamese family meal is enjoyed simultaneously. Every dish complements another: savory cuts the rice, soup clears the palate, and crisp greens ground the feast.

Allergies & Clean Drinking Water Guarantee

We strictly use triple-filtered RO water for all cooking, drinking, and washing. Whether you are strictly gluten-free, peanut-allergic, shellfish-free, or religious vegetarian, our family adapts individual clay pots without cross-contamination.

Our Saigon Family

Why We Opened Our
Family Kitchen to the World

We are Cô Hương (Auntie Huong) and nephew Minh. Our family has inhabited the same leafy hem (alley) in District 3 for over forty years. Growing up, our dinner table was always filled with boisterous laughter, the clinking of porcelain bowls, and the irresistible scent of black pepper caramelizing over charcoal.

When international visitors come to Saigon, they often eat at polished tourist restaurants where the soul of domestic Vietnamese seasoning is tempered. We started Cơm Nhà Người Ta ("Eating at Someone Else's Home") because the deepest cultural revelations happen when you pull up a wooden stool, ask questions without hesitation, and share food cooked from the heart.

Practical Information & FAQs

Everything you need to know before coming to our family table in Saigon.

We meet right at the entrance of Chợ Vườn Chuối (Vườn Chuối Market, Ward 4, District 3). It is easily accessible via a 5 to 8-minute Grab ride from central District 1 hotels (Ben Thanh / Opera House area). Upon booking, we send exact GPS Google Maps pins and a Vietnamese audio note you can show your Grab driver.
Dress comfortably for warm tropical weather! Light, breathable cotton or linen is ideal. We strongly recommend closed-toe flat shoes or sturdy sneakers with rubber soles, as market floors can sometimes be wet from melted ice and fresh produce washing. When entering our home, shoes are taken off as per Vietnamese tradition (we provide clean indoor slippers).
We hold the highest hygiene standards because our family eats the very same meal with you! We only use pure RO filtered water for all food preparation, cooking stocks, and ice. All greens and fresh herbs undergo thorough multi-stage washing with ozone rinses.
You can cancel or reschedule with 100% refund up to 24 hours prior to the experience start time. If it rains (typical for Saigon's afternoon monsoon season), our tours still run seamlessly! Traditional wet markets are fully sheltered under covered awnings, and our home is cozy, air-conditioned, and dry. We also provide umbrellas.
Absolutely! Children aged 6 and above love rolling herbs, shaping rice, and discovering curious tropical fruits like rambutan and dragonfruit. Children under 12 receive a 30% discount automatically applied at checkout.
