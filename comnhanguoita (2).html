The 5-Step Intimate Experience

More Than a Cooking Class.
A Day in a Saigon Home.

From the historic produce aisles of Chợ Tân Định to our family kitchen in District 1, experience the true warmth of southern Vietnamese home life.

1 Step One • ~60 Minutes

Tân Định Wet Market Discovery

We meet at Chợ Tân Định, one of District 1's most iconic wet markets established in the 1920s. We walk past aromatic herb vendors, vibrant mountain vegetables, and fresh catches from the Mekong Delta.

  • Touch and smell Vietnamese herb varieties: *ngò gai*, *húng quế*, *tía tô*
  • Learn market etiquette and respectful communication with local aunties
  • Taste warm banana sticky rice or fresh soy curds along the market path
Chợ Tân Định, District 1
Our District 1 Home Kitchen
2 Step Two • ~90 Minutes

Hands-on Home Cooking

Take a short walk to our cozy alley townhouse in District 1. Tie on an apron, drink freshly squeezed kumquat iced tea, and prepare 4 signature home dishes side-by-side with our family.

  • Master claypot braising (*Kho Tộ*) with caramelized palm sugar
  • Balance sweet tamarind broth and crispy wok-fried greens
  • Hand-crush whole black peppers and shallots in stone mortars
3 Step Three • ~75 Minutes

Dine, Connect & "Mời Cơm"

Gather around the communal family round tray (*Mâm Cơm*). Practice the cherished tradition of *Mời Cơm*—offering respect to dinner companions before tasting the first bite of fluffy steamed rice.

  • Learn chopstick manners and bowl-holding customs
  • Unfiltered stories of Saigon history, everyday family routines, and customs
  • Endless refills of jasmine rice cooked right in earthenware
The Sacred Family "Mâm Cơm"
4 Step Four • ~45 Minutes

Tea, Seasonal Treats & Daily Customs

Relax after the meal with a warm pot of lotus green tea, sliced tropical fruits (sweet pomelo, dragonfruit, rambutan), and handmade mung bean cakes while chatting like long-time friends.

"The table cleanses, but the tea deepens the friendship. Ask us anything about life in Saigon."

Our Saigon Family

Why We Opened Our
Family Kitchen to the World

We are Cô Hương and nephew Minh. Our family has lived in this quiet alley in District 1 for decades. Growing up, our dining table was always filled with laughter, clinking porcelain bowls, and the smell of braised fish caramelized over heat.

International travelers often experience Saigon through restaurant chains. We founded Cơm Nhà Người Ta so visitors could experience what a real Vietnamese domestic meal feels like: eating together, learning about family life, and feeling at home.

Practical Information & FAQs

Everything you need to know before coming to our family table in District 1.

We meet at the main entrance gate of Chợ Tân Định (Hai Bà Trưng Street, Tân Định Ward, District 1). It is easily reachable within 5–10 minutes via Grab car or taxi from Ben Thanh Market, Opera House, or central District 1 hotels. We provide live GPS links upon confirmation.
Dress casually for tropical weather! Flat walking shoes or sneakers with grip are recommended as market walkways can be wet from ice and fresh produce. Indoor slippers are provided when entering our home.
We uphold strict hygiene standards because our family eats the exact same meal with you. We use pure RO filtered drinking water for cooking, stocks, and tea. Herbs and vegetables undergo careful multi-stage clean water rinses.
100% refund for cancellations made up to 24 hours before your session. Tân Định market and our home are fully covered and air-conditioned, so rainy Saigon days run comfortably.
